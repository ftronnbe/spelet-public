Management

Five bosses, one per region

Each one has a pattern, a speech and some last words. They are all, in their own way, administrators.

  1. Floor 3Sir Reginald the Eternally Late“Am I late? ...Oh.”
  2. Floor 6Grumblefoot, Landlord“That is the deposit. Non-refundable.”
  3. Floor 9Duke Gurgle, Lord of Blocked Drains“Mind the... drainage rights...”
  4. Floor 12Madam Shiver, Chair of the Cold Committee“This meeting is... adjourned?”
  5. Floor 15The Hollow King“Mind the invoice. It is on the table.”

The residents

Twenty painted monsters

Four per region. They are walking on the spot here, because this is a website and they have nowhere to be. In the game they very much have somewhere to be, and it is next to you.

The Catacombs Floors 1 to 3

  • Bone Archer

    Keeps its distance. Has opinions about yours.

  • Ghoul

    Crouches, flashes red, leaps. In that order, every single time.

  • Crypt Keeper

    Raises skeletons, then backs away. Basically middle management.

  • Bone Thrower

    Lobs bone bombs. Where the bones come from is not discussed.

The Hollow Caves Floors 4 to 6

  • Shroom Man

    Slow. Swells up when it dies, then bursts. Leaves spores as a parting gift.

  • Cave Troll

    Charges in a straight line. Walls are its natural predator.

  • Goblin Miner

    Throws dynamite, then runs. A coherent career plan.

  • Moss Witch

    Heals her friends, curses you. Fragile, thankfully.

The Sewers Floors 7 to 9

  • Sludge Man

    Splits in two when hurt. Then again. Then you sigh.

  • Rat Catcher

    Three poison darts and a fear of commitment.

  • Bloat Zombie

    Very slow. Vomits, then bursts. You will be standing in it.

  • Sewer Alchemist

    Fire, acid, or a heal for a friend. Chemistry is about choices.

The Frozen Halls Floors 10 to 12

  • Ice Wraith

    Blinks about and chills you. Emotionally, too.

  • Frost Knight

    Shield up front. Go around him. He turns slowly.

  • Yeti

    Leaps, lands, roars. You stand very still for a moment. Not by choice.

  • Snow Shaman

    Hops about drawing lines of ice spikes. Each one warns first. Politely.

The Inferno Floors 13 to 15

  • Imp Summoner

    Summons imps, two at a time. Vulnerable while chanting. Hint.

  • Lava Brute

    Slams the floor. The floor then becomes lava. For a while.

  • Ember Cultist

    Runs at you and explodes. Commitment, at least.

  • Demon Knight

    A warning strip, a sword dash, two cleaves. Very theatrical.

Pick one

Three classes, all called Gerald

Every hero starts as Gerald. You can rename him. Hover over or focus a card to see him swing.

Axes, swords, shields

Barbarian

Real melee, lots of life, and mana that is actually rage: he earns it by hitting things and by being hit. A lunge, a cleave, a slam that stuns and, at the top of the tree, an earthquake.

  • Rend

  • Leap
Bows and quivers

Ranger

Light, quick and always shooting. Fast auto-aimed arrows, a roll to get out of trouble, poison clouds, explosive arrows and eventually a shadow clone who shoots as well.

  • Poison Arrow

  • Rain of Arrows

Sorcerer

Magic missiles as a basic attack and a mana bar to worry about. Fire bolts, a blink, a frost nova, chain lightning, and at the end of the tree a meteor, a blizzard and the Storm Lord.

Took you long enough.Kevin

Party member, technically

Kevin the cat

You find him after the first boss. He has been waiting for three floors and would like you to know that. He follows you down, hides when it gets loud, comes back when it is over and takes partial credit.

He does not fight. His one real value is the hat he wears: each one gives you a bonus while he wears it. You can pet him, within limits. You can rename him, once.

  • Top Hat of Mild ContemptMore gold. Money is attracted to contempt.
  • Party Hat (Unwanted)More experience. He is not celebrating.
  • Crown of the LandlordBetter loot. The good loot pays rent to him now.
  • Bow, Pink, Under ProtestYou walk faster. Out of embarrassment.
  • Halo (Counterfeit)Life regeneration. A small fake miracle.
  • Wizard Hat, Second HandMore mana. The previous owner exploded.

Questions

About, and the FAQ

What is Deep Regrats?

An isometric action RPG for iPhone in the spirit of the classic dungeon crawlers, with painted art and a straight face. You pick a class, go down fifteen floors through five regions, fight twenty kinds of monsters and five bosses, collect loot and find a cat. It plays offline, in landscape, with touch controls.

Is it free?

Yes. The game is free to download and play. It has ads (see below) and one optional purchase, Remove Ads. There is nothing else to buy: no gems, no energy, no loot boxes.

What about the ads?

There are two kinds, and that is all:

  • An optional revive. When you die you may choose to watch a short ad to get back up. You never have to.
  • One ad after a boss floor. When a boss floor's portal takes you back to town. Never in the first five minutes of a session and never more than one every five minutes.

The Remove Ads purchase (29 kr in Sweden, the matching price elsewhere) is a one-time purchase that removes the ads after boss floors for good. The optional revive stays, because it is optional. Ads are shown by Google AdMob: the privacy policy says what that means for your data.

I bought Remove Ads. How do I get a refund, or get it back on a new phone?

Purchases go through Apple, so refunds do too: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We never see your payment details and cannot issue refunds ourselves. On a new or reset phone, open the pause menu, tap REMOVE ADS, then RESTORE PURCHASES.

When can I play it?

A public TestFlight beta is coming, and then the App Store. The buttons at the top of this page will light up when the links exist.

Will there be an Android version?

Not at the moment. Deep Regrats is made for iPhone. If that changes, it will say so here.

Does it work offline? Is my save in the cloud?

It plays offline. Your save is stored on your phone only. Game Center achievements and the depth leaderboard are optional and handled by Apple. Ads need a connection; without one, there simply are none.
